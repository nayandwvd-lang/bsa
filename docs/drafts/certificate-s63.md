# Model s. 63 certificate — electronic records

Section 63(4) of the Adhiniyam requires a certificate in the exact form set out in **the Schedule to the Adhiniyam** (see section 63(4)(c)) before a computer output can be admitted as evidence — full test at [Ch. V part II](/chapters/chapter-05b). This page reproduces the Schedule itself and adds the practical checklist practitioners actually need when filling it in or checking one filed by the other side.

::: proviso This is the Act's own form, not a drafting suggestion
Unlike the other three pages in this toolkit, this is not a model drafted for this guide — it is the Schedule text of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 itself, reproduced verbatim from `reference/bare-act/bsa-2023-full-text.txt`. Do not paraphrase it when filing a certificate; use the wording below.
:::

## The Schedule

**[See section 63(4)(c)]**

### CERTIFICATE

#### PART A — to be filled by the party

> I, _____________________ (Name), Son/daughter/spouse of ___________________ residing/employed at __________________________ do hereby solemnly affirm and sincerely state and submit as follows:—
>
> I have produced electronic record/output of the digital record taken from the following device/digital record source (tick mark):— Computer / Storage Media / DVR / Mobile / Flash Drive / CD/DVD / Server / Cloud / Other: ________________________________________
>
> Make & Model: _______________ Colour: _______________
> Serial Number: _______________
> IMEI/UIN/UID/MAC/Cloud ID: _____________________ (as applicable)
> and any other relevant information, if any, about the device/digital record ____ (specify).
>
> The digital device or the digital record source was under the lawful control for regularly creating, storing or processing information for the purposes of carrying out regular activities and during this period, the computer or the communication device was working properly and the relevant information was regularly fed into the computer during the ordinary course of business. If the computer/digital device at any point of time was not working properly or out of operation, then it has not affected the electronic/digital record or its accuracy. The digital device or the source of the digital record is:— Owned / Maintained / Managed / Operated by me (select as applicable).
>
> I state that the HASH value/s of the electronic/digital record/s is _________________, obtained through the following algorithm:— SHA1: _____ SHA256: _____ MD5: _____ Other: __________________ (legally acceptable standard)
> *(Hash report to be enclosed with the certificate)*
>
> (Name and signature)
> Date (DD/MM/YYYY): _____ Time (IST): ________ hours (24-hour format) Place: ____________

>
> The produced electronic record/output of the digital record are obtained from the following device/digital record source (tick mark):— Computer / Storage Media / DVR / Mobile / Flash Drive / CD/DVD / Server / Cloud / Other: ________________________________________
>
> Make & Model: _______________ Colour: _______________
> Serial Number: _______________
> IMEI/UIN/UID/MAC/Cloud ID: _____________________ (as applicable)
> and any other relevant information, if any, about the device/digital record _______ (specify).
>
> I state that the HASH value/s of the electronic/digital record/s is _____________________, obtained through the following algorithm:— SHA1: _____ SHA256: _____ MD5: _____ Other: __________________ (legally acceptable standard)
> *(Hash report to be enclosed with the certificate)*
>
> (Name, designation and signature)

## Checklist before filing

1. **Both parts, both signatures.** Section 63(4) requires signature by the device custodian **and** an expert — a certificate signed only by one, in the old single-signatory style, does not on its face satisfy s. 63(4). See [Ch. V part II](/chapters/chapter-05b) for why the two-signatory requirement is a real gate, not a formality.
2. **Attach the hash report.** Both Part A and Part B call for a hash value obtained through a named algorithm, with the hash report enclosed — do not leave this field blank or defer it to a later filing.
3. **File it at each instance of tender**, not once for the whole proceeding. A certificate filed at an earlier hearing does not carry over to a later, separate tender of the same record.
4. **Tick the correct device category** and complete every identifying field (make, model, serial number, IMEI/UID/MAC as applicable) — an incomplete device description invites a mode-of-proof objection quite apart from any question about the certificate's substance.
5. **If the party cannot obtain the expert's cooperation**, do not simply file Part A alone and hope — seek a direction from the Court, consistent with the narrow exception discussed in [Ch. V part II](/chapters/chapter-05b).

::: warning Drafting trap
Filing a certificate that tracks the *old* Evidence Act s. 65B(4) single-signatory format — common where a precedent bank of forms has not been updated — will not satisfy s. 63(4). Check the form against the Schedule above before relying on an office precedent drafted before 1 July 2024.
:::
