# Drafting toolkit

These pages are not provision explainers — they are the four documents a practitioner actually reaches for in the run-up to, and during, a hearing where the admissibility or proof of evidence is contested. Each one is cross-linked to the chapter page that supplies the legal test behind it; read the chapter page for the *why*, use this page for the *what to file or say*.

No chapter of the Act corresponds to this page on its own — the roadmap for this guide places the toolkit outside the twelve-chapter sidebar deliberately, since none of the four documents below tracks a single provision.

| Document | Use it when… | Full test lives at |
|---|---|---|
| [Model objection to admissibility](/drafts/objection-to-admissibility) | Evidence is being tendered and you must object *now* or lose the right to object later | [Ch. VI](/chapters/chapter-06), [Ch. X part II](/chapters/chapter-10b) |
| [Model s. 63 certificate](/drafts/certificate-s63) | You are tendering — or opposing — an electronic record and the certificate under s. 63(4) is in question | [Ch. V part II](/chapters/chapter-05b) |
| [Model application to summon a document](/drafts/application-to-summon-document) | A document you need is in the opponent's or a third party's possession | [Ch. V part III](/chapters/chapter-05c), [Ch. X part III](/chapters/chapter-10c) |
| [Cross-examination checklist for challenging authenticity](/drafts/cross-examination-checklist) | You are cross-examining on the genuineness or provenance of a document or electronic record | [Ch. V part III](/chapters/chapter-05c), [Ch. V part II](/chapters/chapter-05b) |

::: proviso
**A checklist is a starting point, not a substitute for reading the file.** Every model document here is deliberately generic — the grounds that actually win a given objection or application come from the specific facts of the case. Adapt, do not paste.
:::
